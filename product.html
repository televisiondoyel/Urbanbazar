Product Details

Experience the untouched taste of nature with Sundarban Honey—ethically hand-harvested from the deep mangrove forests of the Sundarbans, the world's largest mangrove ecosystem. Unlike farm-reared honey, this rare forest honey is collected from wild giant honeybee (Apis dorsata) hives that feed on diverse coastal blossoms like Gewwa, Gorjan, and Khulshi.

Slightly runnier with a characteristic golden-amber hue, it delivers a uniquely floral, slightly tangy taste profile packed with natural enzymes, pollen, and antioxidants.
